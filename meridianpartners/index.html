Why Us

Meridian Communications was founded to address a gap in the market: organisations needed senior-level strategic communications counsel without the overhead and bureaucracy of traditional agencies.

Our founding team brings decades of experience from FTSE 100 corporate communications roles, having navigated high-stakes crises, complex regulatory environments, and challenging media landscapes. We've assembled an international network of handpicked specialists who bring deep expertise across media relations, crisis management, and corporate storytelling. This means you get senior-level counsel, not junior account handlers—every engagement is led by experienced professionals who understand the pressures facing senior leadership teams and the stakes involved when reputation is on the line.

Today, we operate as a lean, specialist consultancy. Our model is simple: connect clients with the right expertise at the right time. No account teams, no layers of management, just experienced professionals who deliver measurable impact and adapt seamlessly to your evolving needs. Whether you're navigating a reputation crisis, crafting compelling narratives for complex organisations, or building long-term reputation resilience, we're pragmatic, responsive, and focused on outcomes. Based in London, we serve businesses, industry bodies and public sector organisations across the UK and Europe.

What we get asked the most!

By taking a strategic approach to communications, we offer clients trusted counsel, ongoing support and deep sector knowledge, protecting their reputation well beyond the headlines.

Strategic Communications

Senior-level counsel to help you navigate complex communications challenges, from regulatory change to stakeholder management. We work alongside your leadership team to develop strategies that protect and enhance reputation while supporting your business objectives.

Crisis Management

When reputation is under threat, clear-headed expertise matters. We provide rapid response crisis counsel, media management, and stakeholder communications to help you navigate your most challenging moments with confidence and control.

Media Relations

Building and protecting relationships with journalists, crafting compelling narratives, and ensuring your story is told accurately. From proactive campaigns to reactive media management, we help you control the conversation and shape coverage.

Corporate & Financial PR

Supporting businesses through transactions, regulatory announcements, and investor communications. We help you communicate complex financial information clearly and compliantly to stakeholders that matter—from analysts to employees.

Issues Management

Identifying potential reputation risks before they become crises. We help organisations anticipate challenges, develop response strategies, and maintain stakeholder confidence during periods of uncertainty or change.

Corporate Storytelling

Helping complex organisations articulate their purpose, values, and impact. We craft narratives that resonate with employees, customers, investors, and regulators—turning corporate messaging into compelling stories that drive engagement.

Interim Leadership

Providing experienced communications leaders to fill gaps in your team. Whether covering parental leave, supporting a transformation, or stepping in during a crisis, we deliver senior expertise exactly when you need it.
