About the Strays Animal Rescue Mission

Strays is a volunteer-run animal rescue dedicated to helping abandoned dogs. We rescue dogs from the streets, provide food and medical care, and help them recover until they find a safe and loving home. Every dog deserves care, safety, compassion, dignity, protection, and a second chance.

Frequently asked questions

Your support helps us rescue abandoned dogs, provide medical care, and find them loving homes. Below are answers to some common questions about donations.

How are donations used?

Donations help cover food, veterinary care, shelter maintenance, and rescue efforts for abandoned dogs in need of urgent help.

Can I make a recurring donation?

Yes, recurring donations help us provide continuous care for rescued dogs.

Can I donate as a foreign supporter?

Yes. Donations from supporters worldwide help rescue and protect dogs in need.

Are donations secure?

All donations are processed securely and go directly toward supporting our rescue work.
